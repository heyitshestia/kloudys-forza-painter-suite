# Community Publishing Policy

## Accepted

- Import-ready KFPS-compatible vinyl JSONs
- A canonical preview rendered from the submitted JSON
- Original work or content the uploader has permission to share

## Rejected

- Source images, local paths, generation reports, settings, or personal data
- Executable files, archives, scripts, URLs embedded in design data, or malformed JSON
- Misleading previews, duplicates, spam, harassment, abuse, or malicious content
- Artwork submitted without permission to distribute it

## Creator Agreement

The uploader retains ownership of their eligible original work. By publishing,
they authorize the community service to host the JSON and preview and authorize
users to download and use it in supported games under the selected upload license.

The service may reject, unlist, or remove material for safety, duplication,
moderation, copyright, or operational reasons. Reports create an auditable review
record. Removal from the active catalog does not guarantee immediate erasure from
external caches or prior downloads.

Uploads pass automated structure, size, canonicalization, preview, hash, and
duplicate checks before publication. Those checks cannot determine ownership,
artistic content, or current game-enforcement policy. Signed-in users can privately
report a listing; open reports are highlighted for the administrator until resolved.

Compatibility labels are derived from the uploaded JSON, not selected by the
uploader. A recognized label means the service understands that source schema; it
does not promise compatibility with every game version. An unrecognized label means
the shape list passed structural validation only, and the uploader must acknowledge
that imports may differ or fail.

Uploaders must classify each listing as **Handmade** (drawn or assembled manually)
or **Toolmade** (generated or converted by a tool). This is catalog metadata, not a
quality or safety judgment. Creators cannot change it after publication; an
administrator can correct a mistaken classification through the moderation console.

## Game Enforcement Disclaimer

Community uploads are not classified as safe for work and are not reviewed or
approved by Turn 10 Studios, Playground Games, Microsoft, or the Forza enforcement
team. Availability in this catalog does not mean an artwork is permitted under a
game's current user-generated-content rules. Users are responsible for deciding
what they download, import, publish, or display in a game and do so at their own risk.
