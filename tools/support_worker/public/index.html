Reports go to the KFPS Support Discord server, created specifically for reporting and resolving KFPS issues. Permanent invite: Join the support server.

Your post will be publicly visible in the KFPS Support Discord server. This includes your Discord display name, post title, affected area, issue description, expected result, KFPS version, report ID, and any screenshots you choose to attach. This is the dedicated support server, separate from the community server.

The remaining technical details stay private. Any additional technical details you choose to include are visible only to Kloudy and authorized KFPS support staff.
