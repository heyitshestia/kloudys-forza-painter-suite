# Night City 2077 Assets

These SVGs were authored specifically for KFPS. They use no extracted game
artwork, logos, icons, characters, or proprietary typefaces.

- `night-city-backdrop.svg`: scalable structural field and service traces.
- `night-city-logo-dial.svg`: angular registration frame for the KFPS logo.
- `night-city-button-signal.svg`: reusable control acquisition marks.
- `night-city-panel-frame.svg`: interrupted technical panel edge.
- `night-city-data-rail.svg`: repeating red/cyan diagnostic rail.
